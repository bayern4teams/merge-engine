## Defect 2 -- the budget cliff (xfail-strict tests, measured)
The switch is a count of unmatched tokens (> 8 per side for equal-size sets, independent of length), not a property of the text. Typo-only sweep, real `_token_ratio`-equivalent similarity:
* 20 tokens: 8 typos 0.925 (STRONG) -> 9 typos 0.600 (DISAGREE); 30 tokens: 0.952 -> 0.624. Same through the real `_comparison_level`.
* Seeded 600-title corpus (9-30 tokens, English vocabulary), flip of level vs the unbounded algorithm: light edits (5 % typo, 5 % drop) 0/600; medium (10 % typo + 10 % drop + 10 % reworded) ~8 %; heavy ~42 %. Direction: WEAK/STRONG true duplicates fall to DISAGREE (e.g. 0.86 -> 0.53). Non-duplicates sharing a few tokens also flip (15 %), mostly downward (arguably an improvement: the soft path rewards unrelated words ~0.3 character credit).
* Harm is confined to roughly 17-35 token texts with >= 9 differing tokens per side (single typo, or <= 8, is never affected; >= 100 tokens, the case D1 targets, loses < 10 % of score). The ponytail comment discloses the ceiling; the report's fix shape endorses exact overlap for long text, but not a count-based switch for mid-length text.
* Suggested fix (not applied): remove the cliff by restricting `token_sim` candidates for unmatched tokens with a character-bigram or first-letter/length-bucket index (report's second option), which keeps typo credit at every length and needs no constant; or make the decision on token count only (accept the step at a stated length).
