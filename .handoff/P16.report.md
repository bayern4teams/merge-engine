## Flag rule (a flag, never a cut)
threshold = half the mean dissimilarity (via `name_variation_rate`) of one name per cluster (the cluster's min comparable name, sampled at stride isqrt(clusters)). A multi-record cluster is flagged when any two of its distinct names are further apart than the threshold (pair = `name_variation_rate([a, b])`, early exit). Fewer than two named clusters: nothing flagged. Only literals 0/1/2 used; the 2 is "half".
Margins on the planted runs: threshold 0.35-0.40, coherent max pair 0.14-0.24, fused cluster pair 0.68-0.82. First attempt (cluster mean rate vs midpoint of median-within and between) had ~0.0 margin on the fused pair and was dropped.

## Changes
1. B-cubed keyed by run: `quality_signals(..., run_id="")`; per entity `{"run_id","clusters","previous":{"run_id","clusters"}|None}` (`_baseline`). Same run_id again compares against stored `previous` and refreshes only `clusters`; a new run_id shifts clusters into `previous`. Empty run_id = no identity (every call is a new run; the old acceptance D tests rely on it). An empty clustering is never saved. Old-format/corrupt files degrade to "not available". `build_reconciliation_report` passes `run_id` (computed once at the top; the existing `"run_id"` return literal now uses the variable, so P17's linkage-literal baseline of 15 stays at 15).
2. Flag rule: names inside a cluster are grouped (union-find) when they share a *significant* token (a token that is at least half the length of one of the names holding it) or one comparable name is contained in another (found with one joined-string `find` per name). Flagged only if >= 2 groups AND the largest group's representative differs from another group's representative by more than `name_variation_threshold` (threshold derivation unchanged, exposed).
   - Deviation from the brief, deliberate: the brief said "share a token". Plain token sharing links "Acme Ltd"/"Orchid Ltd" through "ltd" and acceptance case seed 31 k=3 (two fused clusters sharing "ltd"/"inc") then went unflagged. The "token is at least half of a name" condition ("/ 2", no new constant) fixes that; it also means "Tata Steel"/"Tata Motors" (token 4 of 10 chars) are NOT linked, but the representative-distance test still has to exceed the threshold to flag them.
   - Anchor (largest group) vs every other group, not all group pairs: linear, early exit.
3. Cost: run-level variation now samples each cluster's names at stride max(isqrt(n)//2, 1) (all names while n < 16, so small clusters and the pinned single-cluster equality are exact); threshold sampled the same way. 5000-distinct-name giant cluster: 0.45 s (was 217 s).
