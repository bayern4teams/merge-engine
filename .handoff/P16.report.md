## Flag rule (a flag, never a cut)
threshold = half the mean dissimilarity (via `name_variation_rate`) of one name per cluster (the cluster's min comparable name, sampled at stride isqrt(clusters)). A multi-record cluster is flagged when any two of its distinct names are further apart than the threshold (pair = `name_variation_rate([a, b])`, early exit). Fewer than two named clusters: nothing flagged. Only literals 0/1/2 used; the 2 is "half".
Margins on the planted runs: threshold 0.35-0.40, coherent max pair 0.14-0.24, fused cluster pair 0.68-0.82. First attempt (cluster mean rate vs midpoint of median-within and between) had ~0.0 margin on the fused pair and was dropped.
